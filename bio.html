Longer Biography

I am a research scientist at the Artificial Intelligence Research Center (AIRC), AIST, working on foundational models and 3D computer vision. I am also a visiting postdoctoral researcher at the University of Technology Nuremberg (UTN), collaborating with Yuki M. Asano and supported by the JSPS Overseas Research Fellowship.

I completed my Ph.D. in Systems and Information Engineering at the University of Tsukuba, advised by Prof. Yutaka Satoh. During this time, I also collaborated closely with Hirokatsu Kataoka at AIST. My research focused on scalable dataset construction via formula-driven supervised learning, with applications in 3D recognition.

Prior to that, I received both my M.S. and B.S. degrees in Robotics and Mechatronics from Tokyo Denki University, working with Prof. Akio Nakamura. During this time, I developed a strong interest in computer vision and began my journey as a researcher.

I am broadly interested in machine learning, vision foundation models, generative modeling, and how we can build intelligent visual systems that generalize to diverse real-world settings.
