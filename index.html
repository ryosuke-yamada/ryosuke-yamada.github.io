About Me

I am a research scientist at the Artificial Intelligence Research Center (AIRC), AIST. I am also a visiting postdoctoral researcher at the University of Technology Nuremberg (UTN), working with Yuki M. Asano, supported by the JSPS Overseas Research Fellowship.

Previously, I completed my Ph.D. in Systems and Information Engineering at the University of Tsukuba, under the supervision of Yutaka Satoh, where I also collaborated closely with Hirokatsu Kataoka. I received my undergraduate degree in Engineering from Tokyo Denki University, where I began my research career working with Akio Nakamura. A longer bio is here.
