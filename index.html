About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at the University of Tsukuba, working with Hirokatsu Kataoka in AIST and cvpaper.challenge.
My interests lie broadly at the intersection of deep learning, computer vision, and robotics, with a focus on 3D object recognition.
In my research, I tackle ways in which the automatic construction of a large-scale 3D dataset is based on formula-driven supervised learning.
